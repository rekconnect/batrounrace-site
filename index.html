KM 1 · More than a race

Built by belief, rooted in Batroun.

Created by athletes, engineers, and creatives who love this town — a race with meaning, quality, and community at its core. Open to all of Lebanon.

Community

Everyone runs

Beginners, casual joggers, and elite athletes — teenagers to seniors, locals and runners from across Lebanon.

Cause

Sport with a message

Special editions support charity and community — like our Christmas race, organized to raise donations for communities in need and personal cases.

Coast

The Batroun way

Seaside roads, the old souks, and a Mediterranean finish. After the race, many stay to swim, eat, and celebrate.

Quick answers.

When will I get a reply?

Within 24 hours. Sponsorship & media requests are prioritized around race week.

How do I become a sponsor?

Email us your goals & budget. We'll propose tiered options — route features, jersey placement, on-site sampling, and digital exposure — with measurable reporting.

Where is the race?

Batroun, Lebanon. Start and route maps are shared by email and social.

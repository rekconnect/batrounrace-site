Guidelines: PNG (or SVG — then update the extension in sponsors.html),
transparent background, at least ~400px wide. Logos sit side by side in one
white wall, capped at 56px tall and 150px wide, grayscale until hover. Until a
file exists, the partner keeps its place in the wall as a dashed name chip.

If a logo only exists on a white or coloured square, strip the background
before dropping it here — a solid block reads as a box in the middle of the
wall. `routes.png` is the mark reversed out of its blue tile: the field is gone
and the pin and wordmark carry the brand blue, so it works on light and dark.
